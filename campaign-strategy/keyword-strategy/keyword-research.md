# Google Ads Keyword Research & Strategy

## 1. Objective

The objective of keyword research is to identify search queries that are
relevant to the business, aligned with user intent and capable of generating
qualified traffic and conversions.

The keyword strategy considers:

- Business objectives
- Search intent
- Relevance
- Commercial intent
- Search volume
- Competition
- Expected CPC
- Historical performance
- Conversion performance
- Search Terms data

---

## 2. Keyword Research Process

The keyword research process follows a structured approach.

```text
Business Objective
       ↓
Identify Products / Services
       ↓
Create Keyword Themes
       ↓
Analyze Search Intent
       ↓
Evaluate Keywords
       ↓
Select Match Types
       ↓
Build Ad Groups
       ↓
Create Negative Keyword Strategy
       ↓
Launch Campaign
       ↓
Analyze Search Terms
       ↓
Refine Keyword Strategy
