# Google Ads Campaign Structure

## 1. Objective

The objective of this campaign structure is to create a scalable Google Ads
account based on:

- Business objectives
- User search intent
- Keyword relevance
- Conversion potential
- Budget allocation
- Campaign performance

The structure is designed to provide sufficient control for optimization
while avoiding unnecessary campaign fragmentation.
