### Digital Marketing & Paid Search Portfolio — Praveen Kumar Annepu

---

## 📌 About This Project

This repository demonstrates my understanding of **end-to-end Google Ads campaign operations**, from campaign planning and account structure to tracking, optimization, quality assurance and performance reporting.

The project is designed as a **professional portfolio case study** and uses **synthetic campaign data** for demonstration purposes.

No confidential client, employer or proprietary campaign information is included.

---

## 🎯 Campaign Objective

For this portfolio project, I designed a fictional lead-generation campaign for a digital services company.

### Primary Business Goal

Generate qualified leads through Google Search while maintaining efficient acquisition costs.

### Primary Conversion

- Lead form submission

### Secondary Conversions

- Phone call
- Contact form submission
- Request for consultation
- Brochure/download request

### Core KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Impressions | Measure visibility |
| Clicks | Measure traffic generated |
| CTR | Measure ad engagement |
| CPC | Measure traffic efficiency |
| Conversions | Measure desired actions |
| Conversion Rate | Measure traffic quality |
| CPA | Measure acquisition efficiency |
| Cost | Monitor advertising spend |
| Search Impression Share | Monitor market visibility |
